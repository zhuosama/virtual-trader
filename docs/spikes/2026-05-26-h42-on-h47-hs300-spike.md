**Date:** 2026-05-26
**Author:** claude-code (Mac)
**Budget:** ≤2h hand-rolled (used: ~10 min — answer already on disk)
**Question:** Does the H42 RESEARCH_ONLY verdict change when the price source is swapped from yfinance+mixed to H47 unified Tushare qfq, holding the HS300 universe and gate constant?

## Why this spike was "already done"

H48 was designed precisely as this sensitivity check ("Under a uniformly adjusted Tushare qfq price source, do any H42 candidates flip from RESEARCH_ONLY to passing the gate?" — H48 report § Question). It answered NO on 2026-05-23. The intervening 3 weeks of H49–H52 work was an attempt to **add** signal (sector neutralization, quality factors, risk model overlay, universe expansion) on top of the failing H42 search — not a re-validation of the price-source question.

## Implications for the research line

1. **Original question is closed.** Do not re-run an "H42 on H47 HS300" experiment — the answer is on disk and has been for 3 weeks. Any future Hxx claiming to revisit it must cite this spike + H48 in its Charter and explain what new variable it tests.
2. **Signal is missing, not data.** H47 qfq prices are clean; H42 logic ran fine; the strategy space (24 overlays × 25 params × HS300) simply does not contain a candidate that crosses the gate. Adding sector-neutrality (H49b), quality-value composites (H50b), and risk model overlays (H51b) also failed to pass — all RESEARCH_ONLY with `beat_HS300_windows ≤ 1/5`. The HS300-only search space is exhausted under the current gate.
3. **Next directions are genuinely new questions**, not re-runs:
   - **Lower the gate explicitly** as a research variant (e.g. `beat_HS300_windows ≥ 1/5` + positive deploy excess) — and tag the variant RESEARCH_ONLY-PERMANENT. Outcome: do any of the H48 top-15 become "weak deployable"?
   - **Change the signal family** (e.g. low-vol / quality-momentum / event-driven / cross-sectional ML), not just add overlays.
   - **Change the asset class scope** (CSI500 / CSI1000 / industry rotation) — but only with a new Charter that specifies kill criteria, given H52 was a KILLED line.
4. **Engine + data layer can be frozen now.** Tag the current state as `engine-frozen-v1` per Research Charter v1; no more loader/engine edits in the same PR as a strategy claim.

## Cost of NOT having had this guardrail earlier

The H48 verdict was clear-cut and explicit ("no further price-source investigation needed for this search space"). Without a Charter that says "if H48 says NO, do not search variants of H42 without a new question," the project drifted into H49/H50/H51/H52 — ~20 slices of effort that did not move past `beat_HS300_windows ≤ 1/5`. Charter v1 must encode: **a closed verdict on the original question forces a new question, not more slices on the old one.**

## What this spike DID NOT test

- Different gate thresholds — out of scope; spike held gate identical to H42.
- Different universes — out of scope; HS300 only per question.
- Different signal families — out of scope; H42 search space verbatim.
- Costs / slippage variants — out of scope.
- Walk-forward CV beyond the existing 5-window split — out of scope.

Each of these would be a separate spike, gated by Charter v1.
